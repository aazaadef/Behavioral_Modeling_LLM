# Data attribution & license

The data under `data set/ChildPlay-gaze/` is **not** original to this repository.
It is redistributed from the **ChildPlay-gaze** dataset.

## Source dataset

> Samy Tafasca, Anshul Gupta, Jean-Marc Odobez.
> *ChildPlay: A New Benchmark for Understanding Children's Gaze Behaviour.*
> Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 2023, pp. 20559–20569.
> DOI: [10.1109/ICCV51070.2023.01914](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV51070.2023.01914)

- Official dataset page: <https://www.idiap.ch/en/dataset/childplay-gaze>
- Record / download: <https://zenodo.org/records/8252535>

  here, **unmodified**, for **non-commercial research** and reproducibility.
